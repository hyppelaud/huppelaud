Hüppelaud youth won two awards at Microsoft's global hackathon

In September 2026, Microsoft's Estonia development center opened its doors to outside school students for the first time — and the Hüppelaud-connected team brought home two awards.

September 2026 · Microsoft Development Center Estonia (MDCE), Tallinn

In September 2026, an extraordinary opportunity opened up for Hüppelaud youth: in collaboration with Microsoft Development Center Estonia (MDCE), five Hüppelaud 2026 participants got to take part in Microsoft Global Hackathon 2026. For the first time in its history, MDCE's hackathon included school students from outside the company as well.

Hüppelaud was represented by Konrad Erik Hõbemägi, Emma Saar, Timo Kukk, Kaarel Mihkelmaa, and Elisabeth Käosaar. At Microsoft's Tallinn office, the youth got to work side by side with professional software engineers, learn from industry specialists, and put their skills to the test on real development projects.

A week alongside Microsoft's engineers

Over the week-long hackathon, the Hüppelaud youth contributed to several projects — two of which went on to win awards.

Emma Saar and Konrad Erik Hõbemägi ended up working across two teams at once. Together with Microsoft software engineer Dmitri Kuznetsov, they developed "MDCE AI News Studio," a project centered on building a software solution.

At the same time, the youth worked alongside Microsoft employee Ergo Esken on the hardware project "Black Box." The project brought a different kind of technical challenge than software development, including a motor system that needed precise, not approximate, control.

Beyond the hands-on development work, the youth also got a window into Microsoft's work culture and direct feedback from experienced engineers.

Over the course of the hackathon, Konrad also shared his own experience with Microsoft's development teams. At one meeting, he and Emma got the chance to walk through their own working methods and compare them with the Microsoft engineers' everyday practices.

Two projects, two awards!

The hackathon week ended with outstanding results for the Hüppelaud youth.

The "MDCE AI News Studio" team — Konrad Erik Hõbemägi, Emma Saar, and Microsoft engineer Dmitri Kuznetsov — won first place from the Microsoft Development Center Estonia jury!

And the recognition didn't stop there. The hardware project "Black Box," built with Hüppelaud youth on the team, won the audience choice award, bringing home a second prize.

Microsoft engineer Dmitri Kuznetsov thanked the youth in his own LinkedIn post:

„A huge thank you to Konrad Erik Hõbemägi and Emma Saar for being part of the team and helping turn our Hackathon project into a winning success.“ — Dmitri Kuznetsov, Microsoft, LinkedIn

Along with the awards came the chance to enjoy a celebratory dinner together with Microsoft teammates Ergo Esken and Dmitri Kuznetsov.

A collaboration that opens new doors for young people

For Hüppelaud, this collaboration was an important step toward our goal: taking young people's opportunities further than our own four-day hackathon, and helping them develop their ideas and skills in a professional environment as well.

Microsoft Development Center Estonia also highlighted the collaboration with Hüppelaud in its own hackathon recap:

„We are very glad with the new collaboration with Hüppelaud.“ — Microsoft Development Center Estonia, LinkedIn

What these young people achieved shows that neither age nor limited work experience has to stand in the way of taking part in major technology projects. Given the opportunity and the trust, young people can work alongside professionals in their field and achieve outstanding results.

We're deeply grateful to Microsoft Development Center Estonia for offering our youth this opportunity. We hope it's just the beginning of a long and fruitful collaboration!

Hüppelaud's purpose is exactly this: to be a springboard for young people — a place where first ideas get their start, and where doors open to the next big opportunities.
