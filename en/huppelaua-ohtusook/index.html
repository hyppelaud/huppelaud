Hüppelaud dinner brought the organizing team and mentors together

On the evening of 12 November, the Hüppelaud organizing team gathered with mentors at Alto Rest to celebrate a shared achievement, look back on the past summer, and thank the people who have helped Hüppelaud grow.

12 November 2026 · Alto Rest, Tallinn

On the evening of November 12, the Hüppelaud organizing team gathered with mentors at Alto Rest to celebrate a shared achievement, look back on the past summer, and thank the people who have helped Hüppelaud grow!

Over dinner, we recapped this year's Hüppelaud results. Participant feedback this year hit 9.4/10, and 45% of participants were girls. These numbers show how far Hüppelaud has come, and what a great community we've built together!

We also looked ahead and talked about the upcoming Lumelaud, which will take place this year at EBS in partnership with TooEarly. Also joining us were our great partners Startup Garage and Microsoft, together with whom we've been able to offer young people new opportunities and experiences.

This year marked the 10th Hüppelaud, and the third Lumelaud is coming up soon! The evening was filled with great company, lots of laughter, and exciting discussions about future plans.

A huge thank you to everyone who has been part of this journey. See you at the next one!
